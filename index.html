About Me

Cloud, AI, and the Evolution of an Engineer

With a strong foundation in contact center engineering, I’ve spent years designing, supporting, and integrating enterprise contact center solutions that improve operational efficiency and customer experience. My background includes hands-on work with Cisco UCCE environments, system integrations, automation workflows, and application support in complex production environments.

As cloud technologies and modern application development piqued my interest in recent years, I pursued formal training in cloud platforms and AI-driven solutions. In March of this year, I earned the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner certification and completed foundational and developer-focused coursework in Amazon Connect. By April, I had designed and built an end-to-end AI-powered cloud contact center solution in Amazon Connect, combining cloud services, automation, and conversational AI technologies into a fully integrated environment.

I’m driven by a constant desire to learn, improve, and adapt to emerging technologies. Today, I’m seeking opportunities where I can apply my growing expertise in cloud, AI, and web development while continuing to expand my skills on the path toward becoming a full-stack developer. My experience allows me to quickly understand business needs, contribute effectively, and deliver practical solutions in fast-moving environments. Whether you’re a hiring manager or a fellow developer, I’d love to connect. Feel free to explore my repositories and reach out—I’m always open to collaborating and discussing new opportunities.
